CONTEXT & ROOT CAUSE DIAGNOSIS OF THE TEMPLATES
The system maps web form data into Vietnamese administrative Word templates (specifically "Mẫu 1a-TT.CGCN- Tờ trình CG Tri thức.doc" and "Mẫu 2a-KH.CGCN -Ke hoach CGCN - Trong trường.docx" of Can Tho University of Medicine and Pharmacy). Forensic analysis of the templates reveals the exact root causes of the current export failures:

Binary/Hybrid Format Conflict in Mẫu 1a: "Mẫu 1a-TT.CGCN- Tờ trình CG Tri thức.doc" is a legacy binary OLE2 Word 97-2003 document (MSWordDoc / Word.Document.8) edited with Kingsoft WPS Office (KSOProductBuildVer 1033-11.2.0.9396) containing embedded OpenXML theme fragments. Feeding a .doc file into an OpenXML engine or using lossy HTML/LibreOffice conversions destroys original margins and table layouts.

Uncontrolled Table Autofit & HTML/Code Table Reconstruction: Dynamically rebuilding tables in code or failing to lock <w:tblLayout w:type="fixed"/> causes Word to trigger its "autofit" algorithm. In wide tables like Section 10.1 (6 columns: Stt, Công việc/Yếu tố rủi ro, P, S, R=PxS, Phân loại rủi ro) and Section 10.2 (7 columns: Stt, Yếu tố rủi ro, Biện pháp kiểm soát, Đơn vị phụ trách, Đơn vị phối hợp, Thời gian, Sản phẩm), long text crushes narrow columns and distorts table widths.

Asymmetric Colspan Anomaly in Sections 9.1 & 9.2: In "Mẫu 2a-KH.CGCN", tables 9.1 (Chủ tọa) and 9.2 (Báo cáo viên) have <td colspan=2/> (<w:gridSpan w:val="2"/>) on column 1 in rows 1-2, but on column 2 in row 3 ( | <td colspan=2/>). Cloning rows without removing the mismatched row 3 corrupts <w:tblGrid> column alignment.

Pre-allocated Blank Rows vs. Protected Signature Rows: Tables 8.1-8.3, 9.3 (5 blank rows), 9.4 (3 blank rows), 10.1 (4 blank rows), and 10.2 (3 blank rows) contain pre-drawn empty rows for manual typing. If not cleaned down to a single template loop row, they produce trailing blank rows. Conversely, the two signature tables at the bottom ("Trưởng đơn vị nhận / Trưởng đơn vị giao" and "Phòng KH&CN / Phòng TCKT / Văn Phòng") each have 4 blank rows that MUST BE PRESERVED for physical signatures and stamps.
Conditional Block Gaps & Hardcoded Dots: Section IX ("IX. Hội thảo chuyển giao") is optional ("nếu không bỏ phần IX này"). Inline conditionals leave empty <w:p> paragraphs and blank gaps when Section IX is omitted. Additionally, hardcoded ellipsis/dots (…………) in 6.1 and 6.2 and multiple spaces in Số:           /KH- Khoa/Đơn vị cause text wrapping and misalignment if not properly replaced.
